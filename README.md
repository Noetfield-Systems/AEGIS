# Noetfield AEGIS

Independent product repository for the bilingual Persian/English ISC readiness cockpit and the evolving AI assurance platform.

## Boundaries

- Noetfield Systems Inc. owns this product and its application; TrustField and other ventures remain separate.
- ISC internal readiness estimates are **not** federal scores, approvals, TRL certifications, awarded contracts, or verified business traction.
- Evidence must be real, attributable, reviewed, and limitations retained.
- Governance execution links to Sina Founder OS / Company OS by contracts and receipts, not by duplicating their runtimes.
