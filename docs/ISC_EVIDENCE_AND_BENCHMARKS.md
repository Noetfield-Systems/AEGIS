## Scored criteria (maximum 130)
Q3b advancement beyond state of art (20); Q1b four additional outcomes (10); Q4 technical risk (10); Q5 project risk (10); Q6 18–20 month development plan (20); Q7 team capacity and credentials (20); Q8 inclusion (20, special registered Indigenous business route required for the higher tier); Q9 financial controls (10); Q10 commercialization (10). Threshold >=65 **and every mandatory requirement must pass**. Typical maximum absent qualifying Q8 special route 120/130.
Internal static scenarios: Weak 43, Evidence-built 102, Strong hypothetical 120 — always illustrative, never observed ratings or award probabilities.

## Gates
ELIG: corporate and Canadian eligibility documented.
E1: policy change graph, affected AI systems, historical replay.
E2: vendor-neutral interoperability demonstrated across distinct systems.
E3: role restrictions, residency/retention/export/deletion, cross-tenant negative tests.
E4: >=5 Canadian policy instruments, linked obligations, five-state findings.
E5: human review, reject/annotate, audit history.
E6: first-party data/model/output risk and quality metrics, not external-only evaluators.
M-TRL: actual proposed integrated solution demonstrably TRL5-9.
M-INNOV: non-incremental innovation with reproducible technical proof.
M-SOTA: measured advancement over state of art.

## Benchmark protocol — targets NOT achieved results
Dataset: 30 policy change cases; ~20 AI systems; >=5 policy instruments; 3 adapter contracts; labelled historical assessments; blind independent reviewers.
Outcomes: impact recall >=95%, precision >=90%, reproducibility 100% from fixed snapshots, zero false MET under missing/revoked/tampered evidence, zero unauthorized cross-tenant reads in negative suite, native data/model/output tests with documented limitations, complete before/after provenance, EN/FR reviewer workflow.
Baselines: repeatable manual spreadsheet process + permitted trial comparisons against current commercially available IBM/Microsoft/Credo AI capabilities. Record version/pricing/latency with source; never manufacture numbers.
Study produces versioned fixtures, confusion matrix, intervals, run SHAs, original negative results, human review, adversarial QA, exportable signed receipts. Invalid/old evidence must automatically remove dependent score and gates.
