# AEGIS Product & Founder Intent — Canonical SSOT v1.0
Date: 2026-11-13. Owner: Noetfield Systems Inc. Stage: an operational ISC internal cockpit exists; assurance product is not yet a validated production enterprise product.

## One-sentence thesis
AEGIS transforms policy, AI-model and assurance changes into versioned impact analysis, native data/model/output reevaluation, accountable human review, and independently reconstructable evidence.

## Distinct products
**Readiness Studio**: currently operational internal evidence-led application preparation for ISC challenge EN578-24ISC4/100; scorecard 9 scored criteria and 10 mandatory gates, records of evidence, tasks, milestones, team capacity, interviews, applications and commercialization; private login. No official federal score is generated.
**Assurance Engine**: target customer-facing product for continuous AI assurance, policy replay, source-linked obligations, native risk evaluation, five-state findings, granular permissions, collaboration, change impact, controlled replay and signed export. Do not claim it is implemented by the cockpit.

## Original Founder goals
Excellent bilingual FA/EN premium workflow, practical revenue, defensible benchmarked technical differentiation, low-friction operation, preserve reusable Noetfield IP, independence of other ventures, frequent autonomous supervised development by AEGIS Company OS under Sina Founder OS.
Target customer sequence: Canadian AI firms and regulated mid-market proof pilots; federal departments through official processes; then financial services and international AI governance buyers. Neither an ISC award nor a customer contract is assumed.

## Operational data truth
Evidence must have HTTPS reference, observed date, claim, limitation, reviewer and review time before scoring. Hypothetical evidence is excluded. Uncommitted people remain conditional/unverified. Customer interest is not revenue. Signed authorizations and receipts are not native data/model quality test proof.
Any change to verified evidence affects score, gaps, mandatory gates and action list immediately. Internal score >=65 is never sufficient if mandatory gates are not evidenced.
Formal ISC deadline per scorecard: 2026-12-22 14:00 EST (11:00 Vancouver PST). Confirm official updates before submission.

## Non-goals
Not an AI foundation model, not generic document GRC, not a competitor clone, not a guaranteed compliance certification, not a replacement for Company OS/Runway, not a public multitenant customer portal until tenancy, encryption/backups and access controls are tested.

## Commercial wedge
Fixed-fee AI Assurance Readiness followed by paid 3–5-system Policy Change Impact Pilot, then enterprise annual license, integration and support after customer validation. Prices and ARR are hypotheses until agreements and payments exist.
