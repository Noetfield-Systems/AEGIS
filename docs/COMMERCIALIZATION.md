# GTM & Financial Operating Plan v1.0 (hypotheses)
Customer wedge: AI builders and regulated Canadian mid-market firms with heterogeneous AI systems, recurring policy/model changes and manual assurance review. Federal opportunity is parallel, not sole revenue source.

## Packaging — price discovery, not booked revenue
AI Assurance Readiness Assessment, fixed-fee CAD 3k–7.5k; 1–2 weeks; objective inventory, risk matrix and prioritized proof pack.
Policy Change Impact Pilot, CAD 12k–30k; 4–6 weeks; 3–5 systems; replay results, native tests, before/after outputs, human-review and independent receipt.
Assurance Cloud subscription CAD 24k–72k annual; enterprise private/sovereign edition CAD 100k–240k+ annual plus integration. These are planning hypotheses only; do not display as actual purchase orders.

## Sales workflow
Discover -> qualify buyer authority, review pain, scope -> paid readiness assessment -> pilot proposal -> contract & invoice -> collect real payment -> delivery receipt -> annual expansion -> reference where permitted. No claim of traction without invoice, contract, bank or payment-processor verification.
Target five documented interviews in 30 days and one scoped paid offer sent, with conversion measured. First sale is more important than empty user accounts.

## Economics
Baseline monthly infra under CAD 2k is a target, not observed. Pilot specialists and independent review may cost >CAD 10k/month when staffed. Track delivery hours and gross margin per customer. If manual custom effort dominates, constrain scope rather than pretending high SaaS margins.
Business KPI: collected CAD revenue, booked vs collected, paid pilots, activation, conversion, annual contract value, onboarding cost, expansion, evidence review time, reproducible impact precision/recall, churn and support hours.
