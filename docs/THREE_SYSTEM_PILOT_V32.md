**Status:** commercial offer specification; **not** proof of actual customer contract, invoices or payments. Deliver using the existing company sales, contract, payment and delivery workflows; do not build a duplicate checkout platform just for AEGIS.

## Qualified buyer
Canadian AI builder, regulated mid-market organization or public-sector-adjacent team with at least three inventoried AI/agent systems, a named authorized data owner and a current policy-change/reassessment burden. Legal/permitted access to test data and a customer-designated reviewer are prerequisites for handling *their* data, not barriers to beginning the conversation or selling discovery work.

## Offer / deliverables
**Pilot package:** 3 customer-controlled AI systems, one jointly agreed policy instrument/version difference, an intake and provenance snapshot, first-party native data/model/output suitability checks, affected-system and lifecycle reassessment, a named customer-review checkpoint, a before/after technical dossier and a delivery acceptance call.

**Work boundaries:** no official compliance sign-off; no guarantee of legal applicability; no federal TRL certification or Canada-resident deployment unless contracted and actually verified. Avoid processing unapproved customer secrets. AEGIS current public demo uses synthetic records.

**Commercial price hypotheses, not approved invoices:** preliminary CAD 3,000–7,500 for a fixed-scope readiness diagnostic; a 3-system impact pilot should be individually scoped/quoted after discovery, with acceptance, timeline, fees, access constraints and any necessary independent reviewer costs written in the proposal. Annual licence assumptions are separate and unvalidated.

## Buyer qualification form fields
Organization; buyer title and authority; business/regulatory problem; systems/count and types; exact policy source; available test data and authorization; location and residency requirements; reviewer responsibilities; decision deadline; budget range; procurement requirements; approved communication channel; sales owner; next action and due date.

## Delivery acceptance
1. Exactly three authorized systems imported into an inventory with owner/source/date and validation receipts.
2. Input policy source IDs and evidence snapshots are versioned; unresolved applicability prominently marked.
3. Bounded native run plus policy impact and replay yields source-bound metrics and before/after findings.
4. Client reviewer can approve/reject technical record with traceable identity (requires later independent reviewer work, not currently implemented).
5. Report export and checksum match reproducible run, with limitations attached.
6. Customer records acceptance in writing; invoice state, collected payment receipt and delivery handoff are tracked separately and never inferred.
